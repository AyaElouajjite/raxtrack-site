RaxTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

RaxTrack is a workout tracker that runs on your phone. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Your training data stays on your device

  • Your workouts, sets, personal records, bodyweight entries, goals, body details you choose to enter (height, age, sex, activity level, goal weight), settings and Rax chat history are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • RaxTrack has no account system and no servers of its own. The developer cannot see your training data.
  • API keys you enter for AI providers are stored in the device's secure keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and are only ever sent to that provider.

Advertising (free version)

The free version of RaxTrack shows banner ads from Google AdMob on a few screens (Progress, Calendar and the exercise guide). Ads are never shown during a workout or the rest timer, and never to RaxTrack Pro users.

To serve and measure ads, Google's ads software may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location),
  • device and app information, and how you interact with ads,
  • an advertising identifier, only if you allow it when iOS asks whether RaxTrack may track you (App Tracking Transparency), or on Android unless you have turned off ad personalization,
  • crash and performance data for the ads software.

Your workouts, bodyweight and other training data are never shared with Google or used for advertising.

Your choices:

  • On iPhone, you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you deny it, you still see ads, but they are not personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, RaxTrack asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads, and you can change your choice anytime in Profile → Ad privacy choices.
  • Buying RaxTrack Pro removes all ads.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (RaxTrack Pro)

Payments for RaxTrack Pro are processed by Apple (or Google Play on Android). RaxTrack never sees your payment details.

To know whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID that is created on your device. It does not receive your name, email, or training data. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

AI providers (optional)

By default, Rax (the in-app coach) runs entirely on your device.

If you choose to add your own API key for a third-party AI provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral, Groq, xAI, DeepSeek or OpenRouter) or your own self-hosted server, then when you send a message to Rax, RaxTrack sends your message together with a summary of your training data (recent workouts, records, recovery estimates, goal, split and bodyweight) directly from your device to that provider. You are asked for permission before this happens the first time for each provider.

That provider processes the data under its own privacy policy and terms. You can switch back to the on-device coach or delete your keys anytime in Profile → Rax AI.

Notifications

RaxTrack schedules local notifications on your device: one when a rest period ends, and, if you turn it on, a weekly reminder on the day and time you pick to weigh yourself. No push notification service is used and nothing is sent to a server.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Profile → Backup & export lets you export a full backup (JSON) or your sets (CSV) and share them wherever you choose.
  • Profile → Backup & export → Delete everything permanently erases all RaxTrack data and keys from your device. Deleting the app also removes its data.
  • To ask Google about advertising data, see https://policies.google.com/privacy. To ask RevenueCat about purchase data, see https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Health information

RaxTrack provides general fitness information and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice.

Children

RaxTrack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
