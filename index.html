Frequently asked questions

Where is my data?
On your phone only. RaxTrack has no account. Make a backup anytime in Profile → Backup & export.

How do I restore RaxTrack Pro on a new phone?
Open Profile → RaxTrack Pro and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RaxTrack Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I don't get my weigh-in or rest notifications.
Check Settings → Notifications → RaxTrack, and that the reminder day is set in Profile → Weigh-in reminder.

How do I change ad tracking or consent?
Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone. In the EU/UK, also Profile → Ad privacy choices.

Is Rax medical advice?
No. RaxTrack gives general fitness guidance. See a professional for pain or injuries.
